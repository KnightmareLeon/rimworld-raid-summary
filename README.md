<h1> Overview </h1>

Adds a summary report window detailing a raid when it occurs.
This comes as a letter after the raid letter (i.e. when the raiding pawns have arrived).

The raid summary can be viewed again, it is saved in the messages tab in the history window (the button with the open book icon on the bottom right of the screen).

Can also generate a summary for friendly arrivals (i.e. when your allies help you during a raid). However, this is turned off by default. You can turn it back on in the mod settings.

<h2>Content</h2>

- Raider Faction
- Date and time of raid
- Raid strategy
- Arrival mode
- Total Pawns [includes separate human, animal, mechanoid, and shambler counts(Anomaly DLC needed)]
- Xenotype counts (Biotech DLC needed)
- Equipment counts (melee and ranged weapons), each has sub-lists for quality and material. Also included biocoded counts.
- Apparel counts, each has sub-lists for quality and material
- Animal counts
- Mechanoid counts
